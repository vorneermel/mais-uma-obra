Hand-painted editorial illustration, earthy brick red, golden morning light, blue teal sky, gentle affectionate humor, dignified diverse adult male construction crew, work boots and protective equipment, small detached brick house partly under construction, wheelbarrow, door frame, timber formwork, a thermos and shared coffee on bench in foreground. No hazardous antics, no caricature based on ethnicity, no logos. The house and the friendly workers are main subject in lower two thirds. Upper quarter mostly clean blue sky for typography added separately. No text anywhere. No author name. Not a mockup; full bleed flat artwork. Save generated artwork.
